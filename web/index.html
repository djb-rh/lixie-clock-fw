Enter your coordinates to preview sunrise and sunset.
How do I find my coordinates?

iPhone — Open the built-in Compass app. Your latitude and longitude are shown at the bottom of the screen, under the elevation.
Nothing showing? Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → on, then Compass → While Using the App, and turn on Precise Location.

Android — Open Google Maps and tap the blue dot for your current location. The card that slides up shows your coordinates. (Touch and hold anywhere on the map to get the coordinates for that spot instead.)

Either phone — Standing in the house is plenty accurate. Sunrise and sunset barely move over a few hundred feet.

Paste either format: 38.9072, -77.0369 or 38°54'26" N, 77°02'13" W. Pasting both numbers at once into the latitude box splits them for you.
